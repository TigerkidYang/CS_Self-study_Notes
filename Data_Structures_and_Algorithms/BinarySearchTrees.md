## Motivation

When we are trying to build something, the thing that we care the most would be what the something would be able to do, not how it would do it. You design a phone, you first want it be able to send your message to your mother, as for the way to do it is the problem of tomorrow. Maybe you use some wave thing, maybe link you two by a line, or the phone company just send a guy to your mom's place.
